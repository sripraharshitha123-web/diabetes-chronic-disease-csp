Core Objectives & Survey Pillars

Standardized 32-Question Tool

Direct digital implementation of the official CSP Questionnaire covering 9 vital sections: Demographics, Awareness, Health Status, Lifestyle, Diet, Medical Checkups, Healthcare Access, Daily Impact, and Community Suggestions.

Academic Analytics Dashboard

Interactive Chart.js visualizations tracking disease prevalence, age brackets, rural vs urban disparities, physical activity correlations, and healthcare distance gradients with mathematically defined denominators.

Ethics, Consent & Privacy

Explicit informed consent protocol before questionnaire submission. Zero requirement for respondent personal identity (Q1 is optional), and strictly anonymized data downloads for academic defense.

Read Privacy Policy →

Designed for Rural & Urban Community Fieldwork

The questionnaire is intentionally simplified for respondents across all education levels, while maintaining rigorous epidemiological classifications. Surveyors and community volunteers can complete entries on mobile devices with instant offline-tolerant validation.

Start Community Survey Entry

9 Questionnaire Dimensions:

  • Section 1: Basic Demographic Information (Q1–Q5)
  • Section 2: Awareness of Diabetes & Chronic Diseases (Q6–Q8)
  • Section 3: Health Status & Family History (Q9–Q11)
  • Section 4: Lifestyle, Physical Activity & Habits (Q12–Q17)
  • Section 5: Diet, Sugar & Nutrition (Q18–Q20)
  • Section 6: Checkups, Blood Sugar & Access (Q21–Q24)
  • Section 7: Impact on Daily Life & Expenses (Q25–Q27)
  • Section 8: Community Awareness & Programs (Q28–Q29)
  • Section 9: Prevention Suggestions & Future Willingness (Q30–Q32)
