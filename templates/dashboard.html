Descriptive Epidemiological Analysis & Field Patterns

Key empirical associations observed from the collected community dataset (N = {{ stats.total_n }}).

1. Age-Associated Diabetes Gradient

A distinct upward gradient is observed across progressive age brackets. Respondents aged 46–60 years and Above 60 years exhibit substantially higher self-reported diabetes diagnosis frequencies compared to participants under 30 years.

2. Physical Inactivity & Lifestyle Factors

Sedentary activity patterns ({{ stats.sedentary_pct }}% of total respondents) coincide with higher reported metabolic conditions and infrequent blood glucose testing, emphasizing the need for structured exercise advocacy.

3. Healthcare Distance & Rural Access Disparities

In rural tracts ({{ stats.rural_pct }}% of respondents), distance to primary health centers (> 5 km) corresponds with testing only upon onset of acute illness rather than routine annual surveillance.

⚠️ Methodological Note on Study Design & Causality: All metrics presented represent self-reported community survey responses collected for educational and exploratory awareness purposes. This cross-sectional observational design does not prove clinical etiology or measure general population-wide incidence rates. Figures must be interpreted as descriptive indicators within this sample.
